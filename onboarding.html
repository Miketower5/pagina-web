DO

  • Warm

    Write like you're talking to someone you already know. Familiar, not formal.

  • Honest

    If a roast didn't land, say so. If something changed, explain it. Trust is built by telling the truth.

  • Specific

    Name the farm, the altitude, the barista. Details make content believable.

  • Sensory

    Describe what coffee tastes, smells, and feels like. Let people imagine being there.

  • Local

    Reference the city, the season, the neighbourhood. Make people feel it's theirs.

  • Understated

    Let the work speak. One quiet sentence beats three enthusiastic ones.

DON'T

  • Hyperbolic

    Never say "best", "incredible", "amazing", or "world-class". They signal the opposite of confidence.

  • Generic

    Avoid "a cup of warmth", "your daily ritual", "crafted with love". These could be anyone's café.

  • Corporate

    No mission statements. No "we are committed to". Write like a person, not a press release.

  • Filtered / Fake

    Don't pretend every cup is perfect or every morning is beautiful. Real is more interesting.

  • Trend-chasing

    Skip the trending audio, the dancing reels, the viral formats that have nothing to do with coffee.

  • Pushy

    Don't tell people to "like and subscribe", "drop a comment below", or "tag a friend". Let them choose.
